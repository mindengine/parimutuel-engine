Exacta value

Paste the Exacta probables grid (top row = 2nd-place numbers, each row starts with the winner's number) for real payoffs. Without it, payoffs are estimated from the win pool (Harville) and marked est. Estimates only show where our grades disagree with the board, and when heavily bet F horses are thrown out, most remaining combinations look overlaid. Paste the real probables before acting on an Exacta.

Daily Double value

This race into the next. Leg 2 uses its odds boxes (morning line until you update that race, since its win pool is thin this early). Paste the DD probables grid (top row = leg 2 numbers, each row starts with the leg 1 number).

Value = our probability × what the board pays. Above 1.00 is a positive expectation if our probabilities are right; that rests entirely on the multipliers, which are still being measured. Nothing here places a bet. Odds are only what you paste or type.
