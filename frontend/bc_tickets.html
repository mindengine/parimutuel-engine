How the tickets are built

Trifecta. Win slot: A+ and A only. Hammer: win slot over the A, B[W] and B contenders in 2nd and 3rd. Bombs: same top two slots, with only the C and D horses in 3rd. Scavenger: win slot over the C and D horses in 2nd, anything playable in 3rd. The three never share a combination.

Exacta. A+/A over the A and B contenders, plus a saver with the C and D horses underneath.

Sequences. Main ticket: A+ and A in every leg (a lone A+ can be singled). One backup per leg: that leg's B (and C, D) horses, plus any A left off a singled leg, with the main horses everywhere else. Each backup covers one leg going wrong, and none overlaps another. F and F- are never used anywhere.
